Privacy policy

Last updated 24 October 2026

In short: FabReady does not collect your data. It has no accounts, no ads, no analytics and no internet permission. Everything you enter stays on your phone unless you choose to share it.

What the app stores on your phone

Workshop name and settings, jobs and parts, customer names you type, prices and rates, steel stock and cut records. This is kept in the app's private storage on your device. We cannot see it.

What leaves your phone, only when you choose

ActionWhat is sent and where
Share or print a PDF, image or CSVThe file goes to the app or printer you pick (WhatsApp, email, Drive, a print service). Worker documents contain no customer names or prices; the customer estimate contains the estimate total.
Save a backupA file with the whole workshop, including customer names and prices, is written where you choose. It is not encrypted. Keep it somewhere private.
Send feedback or a "Wrong length?" reportOpens your email or messaging app with a message addressed to us. It includes the app version, phone model, Android version and language, and any feature ideas you ticked. A "Wrong length?" report also includes the part, the length you expected and, if you leave the box ticked, the job's drawing data (sizes, rules and parts; no customer names, prices or stock). Nothing is sent until you press send in your own app.

What we do not do

  • No account or sign-in, and no data is uploaded to a server.
  • No advertising, analytics, crash-reporting or tracking libraries.
  • No access to your contacts, location, camera, microphone or other files. Files are opened only when you pick them (for import or restore).
  • Your data is excluded from Android's cloud backup and device transfer. Uninstalling the app deletes its data; a backup file you saved stays where you put it.

Children

FabReady is a business tool and is not directed at children.

Changes

If this changes, for example if analytics are added, we will update this page and the Play Store data safety section first, and ask for your consent in the app.
