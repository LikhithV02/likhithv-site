Reporting a wrong length

Open the job, tap the part, then Wrong length?. Enter the length you expected and why. The report opens in your email or WhatsApp with the details filled in; nothing is sent until you press send. This is how we fix the rules for everyone.

Backups

Settings, then Save backup, writes a file you can restore later or on another phone. Because the app does not use the cloud, this file is the only way to recover your data if your phone is lost or reset.

Common questions

Does it need internet? No. It works fully offline.

Can I import a list from Excel? Yes. In a new cut list tap Import a list, then paste copied cells or open a CSV file.

Which languages? English, Spanish, Portuguese and Hindi. The app follows the phone's language, or choose one in the phone's per-app language setting.
