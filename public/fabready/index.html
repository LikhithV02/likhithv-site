FabReady

Cut lists for steel fabricators. Enter the parts you need, or a window-grille size, and FabReady plans them from the bars and offcuts you already own, then gives the cutter a clear list to work from.

Works offline. No account, no ads, no tracking.
Your jobs, customers, prices and stock stay on your phone.

What it does

  • Type a cut list or paste one from Excel or Sheets, or enter a grille opening size.
  • Square tube, round pipe, rectangular tube, flat bar and angle, in millimetres or feet and inches.
  • Plans parts from your stock, offcuts first, and shows waste, weight and cost.
  • Records each cut, keeps the measured offcut for the next job.
  • Prints a worker PDF, part labels and a customer estimate. Backs up to a file you choose.
  • English, Spanish, Portuguese and Hindi.

Check before you cut

FabReady is a planning aid. It does not check structural strength, safety codes or installation. Always check every length against your drawing before cutting. Terms of use
