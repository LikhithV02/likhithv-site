Hermes-Jobs

A personal automation tool operated by Likhith V for his own job-search and YouTube workflows.

What it does

  • Gmail: finds and reads job-related messages, creates reply or application drafts, and manages message labels. The current Gmail connector creates drafts rather than sending email.
  • Google Sheets: creates, reads and updates spreadsheets used to track job-search activity.
  • YouTube: reads channel information and analytics, uploads videos privately, and manages video metadata for the operator's channel.

Google access is granted through OAuth consent. The tool uses an AI assistant, so Google data retrieved for a task may be processed by configured AI services. See the privacy policy for data handling, retention and revocation.

This is a private personal-use integration. This page provides app information; it does not offer public registration or access to the operator's accounts.
