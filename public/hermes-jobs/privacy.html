Privacy policy

Hermes-Jobs · Effective 18 October 2026

Hermes-Jobs is a personal automation tool operated by Likhith V. This policy covers its Google account integration and this public information page.

Data accessed and why

  • Gmail: message identifiers, headers, labels, snippets and message bodies, together with drafts created for job-search tasks. This supports finding relevant correspondence, preparing drafts and organizing messages.
  • Google Sheets: spreadsheet identifiers, titles and cell values in spreadsheets the account authorizes the app to access. This supports job-search tracking.
  • YouTube: channel and video identifiers, metadata and analytics, together with video files, thumbnails and metadata supplied by the operator for uploads or updates. This supports managing the operator's channel.
  • Authorization: OAuth tokens and client credentials needed to maintain the authorized Google connection.

AI processing and sharing

Google data retrieved for a task may be included in requests to the AI services configured by the operator. Task content and summaries may also appear in the operator's Discord workspace. Google processes API requests; hosting, messaging and AI providers process information needed to deliver their respective services. Their own retention and security policies also apply.

The app uses Google user data for these personal workflows. The operator does not sell it, use it for advertising, or use it to train generalized AI models. The app's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including its Limited Use requirements.

Storage, protection and retention

OAuth credentials and workflow records are stored on operator-controlled systems. Retrieved content can also remain in assistant conversation history, task files, logs, messages, spreadsheets and backups. Access controls protect the operator's systems, and Google API connections use HTTPS. This is not a claim that all processing stays on those systems.

Records are retained while needed for the operator's workflows and until removed by the operator. No fixed automatic deletion period is promised. Deleting a local record does not necessarily remove copies held in Google, messaging services, AI services or backups.

Revocation and deletion

The account owner can revoke access through Google Account connections. Revocation stops future authorized access; it does not automatically delete previously stored records. Contact likhithv02@gmail.com to request removal of app-held records or credentials. Data stored in Gmail, Sheets and YouTube can also be managed directly in those services.

This information page

These pages contain no forms, analytics scripts, advertising or application cookies. The hosting and network services may process standard request information, such as IP address and request time, to serve and protect the pages.

Contact and changes

Operator: Likhith V · likhithv02@gmail.com. Updates will be posted here with a revised effective date.
